SONG VIEWS — current setup (from views.json + js/songs.js, updated 2026-11-14)

camera:  static = fixed spot, the world moves in front of it
         follow = rides along with a moving subject
         orbit  = slowly circles the subject, one turn per 2-min loop
time:    TW hour / NY hour (NY = TW + 12)
sky:     preset name in the controller (Sky + time)
Controls (Motion card): zoom, camera angle, orbit, Auto orbit (non-stop:
one extra turn a minute, saved with the song) and Pan Left / Center /
Right (glides a tenth of the screen per press, kept when you Save view).
